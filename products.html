Flower, Space Cones, Space Capsules

What we make.

Every Space Weed product starts as Flash Frozen Cured flower: frozen within an hour of harvest, dried under vacuum, with over 80% of its water gone.

For adults 21+.

Where to find it
Deck 02Cargo bay

Cargo bay · manifest

Three products.

Flower, pre-rolls and capsules. Ask your store which strains are on the shelf this week.

  • Flower

    Our flower is different from other brands because it’s Flash Frozen Cured. Removing excess moisture also gives our flower a unique, soft texture, making it easy and fun to break apart; no grinder necessary!

  • Space Cones

    Our space cones are filled with pre-ground flower. Because of the reduced water weight, a half-gram Space Cone looks like a full-gram joint from other brands. More flower, same size.

  • Space Capsules

    Our flower capsules come in two varieties: activated and raw. Both versions are free of chemical solvents, sugar, and other additives.

Deck 04

Same weight. Twice the jar.

Flash Frozen Curing removes over 80% of the water weight. A gram of Space Weed fills the jar like two.

At the counter, ask the budtender to show you the jar next to a conventionally cured gram. You’ll see the difference before anyone explains it.
