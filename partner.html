Three ways in.

Carry it, get certified, or license the process and make it.

  • Dispensaries

    Space Weed is different. But why does that difference matter to you at the dispensary? Our flower turns fast.

    The Space Weed Flash Frozen Cured process is so fast that we can typically go from harvest to shelf within days. Amazing right?

    You maintain ultimate control! We know there’s a trust-building period and we will work with you to design the perfect system for your dispensary.

    Your dispensary’s budtenders take our Space Weed Certification Program on spaceweedusa.com to achieve a better understanding of our unique product. Don’t be afraid to reach out.

    Write to us about carrying it

  • Budtender certification program

    Here at the mothership we value budtenders! Budtenders, want to get Space Weed Certified? We invite you to complete our (very fast!) Space Weed Certification Program.

    Click here to get started/learn more about Space Academy (a separate site)

  • Grower and producer partnerships

    Interested in Flash Frozen Curing, our patented process? It dries flower in days rather than weeks. We are currently in the process of partnering with select growers/producers interested in licensing our technology, the Space Weed brand or both!

    CannaGenesis is the vision of Eric Young. We use science to revolutionize cannabis. We conduct focus groups and use research to develop new techniques, such as Flash Frozen Curing. Four issued US patents cover the method: US 10,639,340, 11,191,797, 11,351,212 and 11,980,304.

    Get the process started, at cannagenesis.com
