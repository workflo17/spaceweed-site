    Bring Space Weed to your dispensary. Flash Frozen Cured flower that sells itself. Our flower turns fast.

    Space Weed is different. But why does that difference matter to you at the dispensary? The Space Weed Flash Frozen Cured process is so fast that we can typically go from harvest to shelf within days. Amazing right?

    We use your sales data to bring you the items that sell through. Your store’s budtenders take our Space Weed Certification Program on spaceweedusa.com to achieve a better understanding of our unique product.

    Here at the mothership we value budtenders! Budtenders, want to get Space Weed Certified? We invite you to complete our (very fast!) Space Weed Certification Program.

    Set the jar next to a conventionally cured gram at the same weight, and let the customer see the difference. The bud breaks up by hand.

    Access Space Academy

  • Growers and producers

    Interested in Flash Frozen Curing, our patented process? We are currently in the process of partnering with select growers/producers interested in licensing our technology, the Space Weed brand or both. The process dries flower in days rather than weeks.

    CannaGenesis is the vision of Eric Young. We use science to revolutionize cannabis. We conduct focus groups and use research to develop new techniques, such as Flash Frozen Curing. Four issued US patents cover the method: US 10,639,340, 11,191,797, 11,351,212 and 11,980,304.
