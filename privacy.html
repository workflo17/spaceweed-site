What is stored.

  1. No cookies

    This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from advertising or analytics companies. Its fonts, pictures and footage are served from the site itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs.

  2. In your browser

    A few small notes sit in your browser, and none of them is sent anywhere. Your answer to the age question is kept until you close the tab, so you are not asked on every page; a new visit asks again. While the question is asked, a note of the page you were going to waits in the same tab. If you use the Motion switch, your choice is kept as well. A note of which door you used between pages lasts only until the next page opens. If you have closed or used the sign-up window, a note of that is kept for 30 days (a year, once you have used it). If you sign up on the Classified page, the Space Cadet card it shows you is kept in your browser until you clear it there. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them all.

  3. Your email

    If you email info@spaceweedusa.com, your message is handled by the Space Weed team. The contact form (on the Contact page, at the foot of the Partner page, and in the window that Mission Control opens) and the sign-up window open your own mail app; they send nothing from here.
