What is stored.

This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from advertising or analytics companies. Its fonts, pictures and footage are served from the site itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs.

One value is stored in your browser: your answer to the age question, kept for 30 days so you are not asked on every page. Clearing your browser’s site data removes it.

If you email info@spaceweedusa.com, your message is handled by the Space Weed team. The contact page’s form opens your own mail app; it sends nothing from here. The store-name form on the find page does the same: it writes the email in your browser, sends nothing from this site, and keeps nothing you type.
