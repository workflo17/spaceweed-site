Learn · the science behind Space Weed

What is Flash Frozen Cured?

Flash Frozen Curing is a patented scientific method whereby fresh cannabis is flash frozen and then dried under vacuum using temperature, time, and pressure.

Five things to know

  • Fresher

    Just picked off the plant

    Space Weed is Flash Frozen Cured to preserve cannabis in its freshest state. Space Weed maintains its “just picked off the plant” qualities. This means bigger and greener buds.

  • Cleaner

    Temperature, time and pressure

    Flash Frozen Curing uses temperature, time and pressure.

  • Smoother

    Moisture removed

    Space Weed burns smoother since the moisture is removed during Flash Frozen Curing.

  • Softer

    No grinder needed

    Space Weed has a uniquely soft texture which makes it super easy and fun to break apart.

  • More weed

    Less H2O content

    Flash Frozen Curing removes excess moisture from the bud. Since water is so heavy, Space Weed has twice the volume of normal weed.

Products · the one thing

2x more bud. Same weight.

Most cannabis is dried by hanging for weeks. Space Weed is frozen within an hour of harvest and dried under vacuum.

Flash Frozen Curing removes excess moisture from the bud. Since water is so heavy, Space Weed has twice the volume of hang-dried flower.
