Cargo bay · the one thing

2x more bud. Same weight.

Most cannabis is dried by hanging for weeks. Space Weed is frozen within an hour of harvest and dried under vacuum.

Flash Frozen Curing removes excess moisture from the bud. Since water is so heavy, Space Weed has twice the volume of hang-dried flower.

Engine room · how it works

Ice to vapour, never liquid.

In a vacuum, ice skips melting and goes straight to vapour.

Flash Frozen Curing is a patented scientific method whereby fresh cannabis is flash frozen and then dried under vacuum using temperature, time, and pressure.

The window: comet 67P, photographed by ESA’s Rosetta on 10 September 2015. Sunlight warms the comet’s ice and the ice turns straight to vapour, carrying dust out in jets. Slowed, looped and tinted in processing.

The archive · the record

Four issued US patents.

All four are titled “Method of drying botanicals”, with Eric Young as the inventor, and all four trace back to one provisional application filed on 18 June 2018. The first was granted on 5 May 2020. They are public record at the US Patent and Trademark Office.

Observation deck · ship’s log

Why it’s called Space Weed.

We got our name from our early supporters, who compared the texture of our Flash Frozen bud to astronaut ice cream. Space Weed is the product of our unique Flash Frozen Curing process. Learn more about that here. Space Weed is one of the only truly different cannabis products on the market.

Cannabis defined itself in the era of prohibition, as an element of counter-culture. Those days are gone, but much of what cannabis is and how it’s produced and consumed are still defined by antiquated techniques. There’s no reason that inertia should inhibit cannabis development. We believe that science needs to be the new guiding light.

Through the porthole: “The Blue Marble”, photographed by the crew of Apollo 17 on 7 December 1972. NASA.
